CONTEXT — Inclinations!

What this is. A book titled Inclinations! — A road to the Singularity, comprising 46 plates plus a wraparound cover. The plates correspond to the 46 supplied poster subjects, from PEDLER and the Walk through inclination fields, physical AI, CEMs/CEMb, language, chemistry and biology, quantum and gravitational correlations, and the synthesis architecture.

Book scope. The work treats PEDLER as a foundational transition calculus; the Walk as realised trajectories through configuration space; inclination as the directional primitive of transition; and an inclination field as a field of such signed transition dispositions. It extends these primitives into signals and systems, state spawning, semantics, chemistry, biochemistry, genetics, evolution, linguistic vector fields, ILM, physical AI, CEMs, CEMb, Quantum Neuromorphic BCI, Eco-CEM, and the proposed convergence of bioform and synthetiform cognition.

Primitives a reader or agent must preserve before translating. Inclination is signed and lives on transitions/edges. It is not to be collapsed into gradient, probability, intent, Act, or execution. A Walk is the effect of inclinations between states. State spawning permits an apparently single transition to contain previously unresolved intermediate or intermittent states. An Act is executable intent, and Natural Justice gates intent → execution where that ontology is invoked. Convergence is treated as contraction of a signed field rather than selection from an unsigned set.

Cross-domain register. Correlations and hypotheses must remain correlations and hypotheses. In particular, proposed correspondences involving neuroscience, wave–particle duality, collapse, gravity/quantum gravity, chemistry-to-biology derivations, quantum neuromorphics, and convergence must not be silently promoted to established physical results.

Physical-AI provenance. NI2A2, ANGEL, TARA and the later physical-AI lineage are historical engineering work and must remain distinct from later AI-generated summaries or reconstructions. The physical laboratory record is an authoritative source still to be recovered where it has not been incorporated into the book.

Linguistic architecture. LVF denotes Linguistic Vector Fields. ILM denotes Integrative Linguistic Multiscript. Translation, transliteration, language development and language evolution are treated as candidate vector-field/transition-field domains without collapsing linguistic structure into generic probability.

CEM architecture. CEMs denotes synthetiform realization, including AI acceleration, conventional matrix/tensor acceleration, neuromorphic realization and possible quantum-neuromorphic realization. CEMb denotes bioform realization, including neural decoding and semantic cognitive-state recovery. Quantum Neuromorphic BCI is the proposed bidirectional bridge. CEMs and CEMb are parallel realization regimes sharing semantic structure rather than mandatory sequential stages. Eco-CEM is a parallel ecological realization path.

Convergence. The convergence architecture is expressed through shared Intent/semantic structure, refinement, verification/equivalence and bidirectional realization. The proposed Singularity is convergence of bioform and synthetiform cognition; it must not be described as an established physical event.

Interpretive Working Basis. IWBT concerns preservation of the subject/system's working basis during interpretation. Flattening, proxy substitution, ontology substitution or frame switching can distort cognitive or linguistic outliers and must not be introduced silently.

Canonicality. The plates are canonical artifacts. Later defects or omissions are handled by new versions/plates rather than silently rewriting the historical artifact. Source-grounded recovery takes precedence over reconstruction.
