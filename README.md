Inclinations!

A road to the Singularity

A book of 46 plates plus a wraparound cover tracing a transition from PEDLER's foundational transition calculus to a broader architecture of inclination fields, embodiment, language, biology, computation and convergence.

Core primitives

PEDLER represents configurations and states connected by transitions.

Inclination is a signed directional disposition over possible transitions, attached to the transition structure rather than to states.

The Walk is the realised trajectory produced by inclinations between states.

State spawning permits an apparently single transition at one observational resolution to resolve into intermediate or intermittent states at a finer resolution.

The work preserves distinctions among inclination, gradient, probability, intent, Act and execution. Where the Natural Justice architecture is invoked, Natural Justice gates intent → execution.

Epistemic register

Cross-domain correspondences are not automatically established equivalences. Where a plate identifies a result as a hypothesis or correlation, that status is preserved. In particular, neuroscience correlations, biomimetic claims, wave–particle/collapse correspondences, gravity/quantum-gravity proposals, and quantum-neuromorphic convergence remain framed at the level stated by the source.

The physical-AI lineage is historical engineering work, not a reconstruction invented from the poster series. Source notes and laboratory records remain authoritative wherever they exist.
